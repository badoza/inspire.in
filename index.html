Our Story[cite: 4]

IT STARTED WITH A BASKETBALL AND A BELIEF.[cite: 4]

Founded by Santosh and Manjunath Hullur in January 2022, Inspire Basketball Academy & Club was built on one simple belief: every child deserves the opportunity to discover and develop their potential.[cite: 4]

Our journey began with the belief that sport is about much more than winning. Basketball can help children build confidence, discipline, resilience, character and lifelong friendships.[cite: 4]

At Inspire, we are not just building basketball players. We are helping build people for life.[cite: 4]

FIVE PROGRAMMES.
ONE LOVE FOR BASKETBALL.[cite: 4]

Our programmes give children and young adults opportunities to learn, improve and compete. Age groups are guidelines. Programme placement and progression depend on each player's ability, experience and readiness.[cite: 4]

01 6–9 Years[cite: 4]

ROOKIE | PRE-BEGINNER[cite: 4]

Discover. Move. Play.[cite: 4]

A fun introduction to basketball for young children beginning their sporting journey. Through age-appropriate activities, children become comfortable with the ball, enjoy movement and develop confidence.[cite: 4]

  • Movement, balance and coordination.[cite: 4]
  • Ball familiarity and basic ball control.[cite: 4]
  • Listening, sharing and following instructions.[cite: 4]
02 10–12 Years[cite: 4]

RISING STAR | BEGINNER[cite: 4]

Learn. Practise. Improve.[cite: 4]

A structured introduction to the essential skills and habits of basketball. Through consistent practice, players develop sound techniques and a better understanding of the game.[cite: 4]

  • Dribbling, ball control and passing.[cite: 4]
  • Shooting technique and basic finishing.[cite: 4]
  • Teamwork, communication and sportsmanship.[cite: 4]
03 13–16 Years[cite: 4]

PRO | INTERMEDIATE[cite: 4]

Understand. Adapt. Compete.[cite: 4]

Players progress to more challenging training that connects individual skills with team play. The focus is on applying techniques, making decisions and responding to different game situations.[cite: 4]

  • Advanced ball-handling and shooting consistency.[cite: 4]
  • Game reading and decision-making.[cite: 4]
  • Match situations and competitive experience.[cite: 4]
04 17–22 Years[cite: 4]

ELITE | ADVANCED[cite: 4]

Prepare. Perform. Progress.[cite: 4]

A focused development programme for committed and suitably prepared players aspiring to take their basketball performance to a higher level. Training emphasises technical skills, tactical understanding, physical preparation and competitive readiness.[cite: 4]

  • Advanced technical and tactical development.[cite: 4]
  • Position-specific skills and game responsibilities.[cite: 4]
  • Competitive mindset, leadership and responsibility.[cite: 4]
05 All Ages[cite: 4]
