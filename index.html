About Inspire

WHO WE ARE

INSPIRE Sports Academy is a sports-development organisation committed to helping people become better through sport. We focus on personal growth, athletic development and competitive excellence through professional coaching, structured programmes and progressive pathways.

We bring together players, coaches, families and institutions to create a strong, inclusive and sustainable sporting ecosystem.

Our flagship programme is Inspire Basketball Academy. Our long-term ambition is to grow into a broader sports-development ecosystem covering multiple sports.

Our Vision

To inspire every child to discover, develop and cultivate their sporting potential from an early age.

Our Mission

To provide every child with the opportunity, environment and pathway to discover, develop, build, compete and excel in sport and life.

Our Story

IT STARTED WITH A BASKETBALL AND A BELIEF.

Founded by Santosh and Manjunath Hullur in January 2022, Inspire Basketball Academy & Club was built on one simple belief: every child deserves the chance to discover and develop their potential.

Our journey began with the conviction that sport is about much more than winning. Basketball can help children build confidence, discipline, resilience, character and lifelong friendships. Along the way, a committed leadership team backed our vision and contributed their time, experience and support. Their trust helped turn a shared dream into reality.

Today, that belief is reflected in our own FIBA-standard court in Hubballi–Dharwad. Guided by a child-first philosophy, we are creating opportunities for young players to learn, improve and pursue their ambitions.

The court is ready. The dreams are bigger. Our journey is just beginning. At Inspire, we are not just building basketball players, but helping build people for life.

FIVE PROGRAMMES.
ONE LOVE FOR BASKETBALL.

Our basketball programmes cater to different ages, abilities and experience levels, from first-time beginners to competitive and recreational players. Age groups are guidelines. Placement and progression depend on each player's ability, experience and readiness.

01 6–9 Years

ROOKIE | PRE-BEGINNER

Discover. Move. Play.

This is where a child’s basketball journey begins. Through fun games and simple activities, children learn to move, balance, coordinate and become comfortable with the ball. The focus is on building confidence, making friends and discovering the joy of playing basketball.

02 10–12 Years

RISING STAR | BEGINNER

Learn. Practise. Improve.

Ready to learn the basics? Players build a strong foundation in dribbling, passing, shooting, footwork and simple game rules. With regular practice and encouragement, they develop better control, teamwork and the confidence to enjoy playing with others.

03 13–16 Years

PRO | INTERMEDIATE

Understand. Adapt. Compete.

For players who know the basics and are ready to take their game further. Training helps them use their skills in real match situations, make smarter decisions, understand team play and improve both attack and defence. Players also build fitness, resilience and confidence through regular practice and competitive experience.

04 17–22 Years

ELITE | ADVANCED

Prepare. Perform. Progress.

Designed for committed players who want to push their performance to the next level. Training focuses on advanced skills, game strategy, position-specific roles and athletic development. Players work towards greater consistency, stronger leadership and readiness for higher-level competition, based on their ability, performance and selection.

05 All Ages

BASKETBALL FOR LIFE

Play. Move. Connect. Enjoy.

Basketball is not only for competitive players—it is a game for life. Whether you are new to the sport, returning after a break, a working professional or a parent, this programme gives you a chance to stay active, improve your fitness, make friends and enjoy the game at your own pace.

Our Player Development Pathway

DISCOVER. DEVELOP. BUILD. COMPETE. EXCEL.

We believe every child has potential. With the right coaching, environment, commitment and opportunities, every player can take meaningful steps towards becoming their best. These are stages of development, not fixed age groups. Each player progresses according to individual readiness.

DISCOVER

Fall in love with the game.

  • ■ Experience basketball through fun, engaging activities.
  • ■ Develop movement, coordination and ball familiarity.
  • ■ Build confidence and a positive attitude towards learning.

DEVELOP

Build strong fundamentals.

  • ■ Learn dribbling, passing, shooting and footwork.
  • ■ Improve ball control and basic game understanding.
  • ■ Gain consistency through structured practice.

BUILD

Become a stronger athlete.

  • ■ Develop strength, speed, agility and coordination.
  • ■ Improve balance, mobility and movement quality.
  • ■ Strengthen discipline, resilience, confidence and teamwork.

COMPETE

Put skills into action.

  • ■ Apply skills in real-game situations.
  • ■ Develop decision-making and team strategies.
  • ■ Gain match experience through available competitive opportunities.

EXCEL

Strive for your potential.

  • ■ Pursue advanced technical and tactical development.
  • ■ Prepare for higher levels of competition.
  • ■ Explore club, district, state and national opportunities, subject to eligibility, performance and selection.
Our Approach to Every Player

STRUCTURED. PROGRESSIVE. PLAYER-FOCUSED.

We recognise that every player is different. Our coaching matches learning to age, ability, experience and readiness. Training combines technical skills, physical development, tactical learning and game play. We focus on strong fundamentals, teamwork, enjoyment and individual progress.

HOW OUR TRAINING WORKS

■ Age-appropriate learning: Skill-based training and activities suited to each player's development.

■ Individual growth: Personal progress within an inclusive team environment.

■ Learn through play: Practice games and competition give players the chance to apply their skills.

■ Consistent training: Regular coaching sessions planned across the week.

■ Balanced development: Training designed to support sporting growth alongside children's academic commitments.

AT INSPIRE DEVELOPMENT IS A JOURNEY – NOT A RACE

We don't rush development: we nurture potential, recognise individual progress and help every player take the next step.
